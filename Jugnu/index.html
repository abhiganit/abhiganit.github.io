Space and rockets

Eight books, in order, each one taking a single problem that stood between people and the sky — and the trick that got past it. From how far away the Moon really is, to landing a booster back on its own fire.

Maps and the world

An atlas a child can use before they can read. Turn on one thing at a time — mountains, rivers, volcanoes, countries — tap any of it, and it says out loud what it is and how big.

632 mountains · 1,558 volcanoes · 1,000 rivers · it talks

More will appear here as he gets interested in more things. That is the whole plan.
